Dave Stevens' 1982 comic, and the 1991 Disney film, follows a Depression-era stunt pilot who discovers a mysterious jetpack and becomes a vigilante. The jetpack is the purest expression of aeromythic desire: flight without vehicle, without wing, without anything between the body and the sky except thrust.

## Real Jetpacks

Bell Aerosystems developed the "Bell Rocket Belt" for the U.S. Army in the early 1960s, a hydrogen peroxide-powered device capable of approximately 30 seconds of flight. It appeared in James Bond's *Thunderball* (1965) and at the 1964 World's Fair. The dream of the personal jetpack has been "10 or 20 years away" for over 60 years.
