Specialized nicknames more common in the tactical jet community, but used across military aviation and NASA. Callsigns are typically based on first or surname and replace a pilot's given name in operational contexts.

The callsign is a small myth — a persona granted by the community, not chosen by the individual. You don't pick your own callsign. It is assigned, usually based on a memorable incident, a name pun, or a personality trait the group decides to fix onto you permanently.

## Fiction and Callsigns

*Top Gun*'s callsigns — Maverick, Goose, Iceman — function as archetypes, not names. Maverick is the rule-breaker. Goose is the loyal sidekick. Iceman is pure technical excellence without warmth. They are the Aeromythic Archetypes in flight suits.

Hal Jordan, Green Lantern, flew as "Highball" before becoming a space cop. The callsign bridges the human pilot and the cosmic superhero.
